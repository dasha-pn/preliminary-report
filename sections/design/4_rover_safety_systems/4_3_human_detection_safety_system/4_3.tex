The system is based on HLK-LD2450 human presence sensor,
each providing simultaneous distance and angular position measurements of detected targets.
The sensor operates through UART communication, on \qty{5}{\volt} power, \qty{3.3}{\volt} logical voltage, with a \ang{120} field of view and detects human presence up to \qty{6}{\metre} in open environments.
The positional accuracy is within \qty{0.1}{\metre}, enabling precise localization relative to the rover.

Each sensor can track up to three moving individuals, provided a minimum separation of approximately \qty{1}{\metre}.
Targets closer than this threshold may be merged into a single detection.
The radar operates independently of lighting conditions and is robust to dust and partial occlusions, ensuring reliable performance in outdoor environments.

A total of four sensors are mounted to provide full \ang{360} coverage around the rover, with overlapping fields of view to eliminate blind spots.
Sensor data is processed in real time to estimate relative position and direction of detected humans.

An emergency stop is triggered when a human is detected within a predefined safety radius of \qty{4}{\metre}.

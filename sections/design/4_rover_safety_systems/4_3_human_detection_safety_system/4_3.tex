The system utilizes four HLK-LD2450 human presence sensors mounted to provide full \ang{360} coverage with overlapping fields of view, eliminating blind spots. Each sensor features a \ang{120} field of view and tracks up to three moving individuals up to \qty{6}{\metre} away with a positional accuracy of \qty{0.1}{\metre}. 

Operating independently of lighting conditions and robust against dust and partial occlusions, the radar ensures reliable outdoor performance. The system processes data in real time to estimate the relative distance and angular position of detected targets, triggering an emergency stop if a human enters a predefined \qty{4}{\metre} safety radius.

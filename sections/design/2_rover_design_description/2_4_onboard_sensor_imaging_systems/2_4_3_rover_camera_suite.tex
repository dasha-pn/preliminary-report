An Arducam AR0234 color global shutter USB 3.0 camera is mounted \qty{10}{\centi\metre} above the gripper, aligned parallel to its orientation to support object recognition and pose estimation. The global shutter sensor ensures blur-free imaging during dynamic arm movements. The module utilizes an M12 manual-focus lens ($f = \qty{3.6}{\milli\metre}$) delivering an \ang{82}~$\times$~\ang{66} (H$\times$V) FoV with low ($\sim$\qty{3}{\percent}) distortion.

The rover's camera suite consists of a stereo pair main color camera, front and rear facing wide-angle color cameras, providing comprehensive visual information for navigation. The stereo pair captures a \numproduct{2560 x 720} \qty{93}{fps} video for clear imaging, while the wide-angle cameras offer a wider field of view for situational awareness and terrain assessment in front of the rover. The main camera has a \qty{120}{degree} and a \qty{90}{degree} field of view for the left and right cameras, respectively, while the wide-angle cameras have a \qty{150}{degree} field of view.
